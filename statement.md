# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Emergency rooms attend to patients according to different levels of urgency, and the patients' conditions, the availability of doctors, and the number of new patients can all vary with time; a method based on a fixed queue does not properly reflect these changing circumstances.
The Emergency Room Decision Simulator addresses this problem by simulating patient arrival, dynamic priority assessment, patient deterioration, doctor allocation, treatment, and event processing in a Java-based environment

## Scope of the Project

The project focuses on simulating the decision-making and resource allocation process within an emergency room.
The system includes:
- Patient registration and validation
- Dynamic triage based on symptoms and vital signs
- Priority-based emergency queue management
- Doctor allocation based on specialization
- Simulation of patient deterioration
- Event-driven treatment processing
- Multithreaded simulation
- Statistics and report generation
- File-based storage of patient history and simulation reports

## High-Level Features

1. **Patient Management** – Register and validate patient information.
2. **Dynamic Triage** – Calculate patient priority using symptoms and vital signs.
3. **Emergency Queue** – Organize patients according to their priority.
4. **Doctor Allocation** – Assign available doctors according to specialization.
5. **Event-Driven Simulation** – Process patient arrivals, deterioration, treatment completion, and doctor availability.
6. **Multithreading** – Simulate concurrent activities within the emergency room.
7. **Statistics & Reporting** – Track waiting time, treatment time, patient outcomes, and doctor workload.
8. **File Handling** – Store patient history and generated simulation reports.

### The project is intended as an educational simulation and is not designed for real-world clinical decision-making.
